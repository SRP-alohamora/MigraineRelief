# Oral Triptans (Sumatriptan 100mg, Rizatriptan 10mg)

## Pharmacodynamics
5-HT 1B/1D selective agonism producing vasoconstriction of painfully dilated meningeal dural vessels and presynaptic trigeminal neuropeptide inhibition.

## Pharmacokinetics
- **Rizatriptan 10mg**: T_max 1.0–1.5 hours. 2-Hour Pain Freedom ~67%.
- **Sumatriptan 100mg**: T_max 2.0–2.5 hours. Bioavailability ~14% (oral first-pass).

## Crucial Requirement: Gastric Motility
Oral tablets require active gastric emptying to reach the duodenal lumen where absorption occurs. If gastric stasis / nausea is present, absorption is delayed by 120–180 minutes or abolished.
