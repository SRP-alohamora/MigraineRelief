# Dihydroergotamine Precision Olfactory Delivery (DHE POD / INP104)

## Mechanism of Action
Multireceptor ergot alkaloid acting as an agonist at 5-HT 1B, 1D, 1F, and alpha-adrenergic receptors. Displays prolonged neurovascular dissociation half-life, resulting in very low 24-hour and 48-hour headache recurrence rates.

## Pharmacokinetics & Bioavailability
- **Administration**: Upper nasal space delivery targeting the vascularized olfactory cleft.
- **T_max**: 20–30 minutes.
- **Recurrence Rate**: <15% within 48 hours (vs ~30-40% for oral triptans).

## Clinical Indications
- Refractory chronic migraine (e.g. Persona 2: Claire Sterling).
- Attacks awakening patient from sleep (>2 hours elapsed duration).
- Central sensitization / cutaneous allodynia locked-in state.

## Contraindications & Age Gates
- Strictly contraindicated in pediatric and adolescent patients (<18 years).
- Coronary artery disease, peripheral vascular disease.
- Pregnancy (Category X).
- CYP3A4 strong inhibitors (e.g., macrolides, protease inhibitors, ketoconazole).
