# Small-Molecule CGRP Receptor Antagonists (Gepants)

## Agents & Formulations
- **Rimegepant 75mg**: Oral disintegrating tablet (ODT). Dual acute rescue and every-other-day prevention.
- **Zavegepant 10mg**: Intranasal spray. Rapid non-oral CGRP antagonist.
- **Ubrogepant 50mg/100mg**: Oral tablet.

## Clinical Advantage: Cardiovascular Safety
Unlike triptans and ergots, gepants do NOT cause vasoconstriction. Safe in patients with coronary artery disease, history of stroke, or peripheral vascular disease.
