# Sumatriptan Subcutaneous Auto-Injector (6mg / 0.5mL)

## Mechanism of Action
Selective 5-HT 1B/1D receptor agonist. Induces cranial vasoconstriction and inhibits calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) release from trigeminal sensory nerve endings.

## Pharmacokinetics & Bioavailability
- **Bioavailability**: 97% (bypasses gastrointestinal hepatic first-pass metabolism).
- **T_max**: 10–12 minutes.
- **2-Hour Pain Freedom**: 82% (highest acute single-agent response in Cochrane systematic reviews).

## Clinical Indications
- Acute severe migraine attacks with early or rapid progression.
- Attacks complicated by severe nausea or emesis (gastric stasis).
- Failure of oral triptan therapy.

## Contraindications
- Ischemic coronary artery disease (CAD), previous myocardial infarction, angina.
- Cerebrovascular accidents (stroke/TIA).
- Hemiplegic or basilar migraine.
- Uncontrolled hypertension.
- Concurrent use within 24 hours of another triptan or ergotamine derivative.
