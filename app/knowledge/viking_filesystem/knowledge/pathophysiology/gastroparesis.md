# Migraine-Induced Gastric Stasis and Gastroparesis

## Clinical Evidence (Aurora et al., 2022)
Acute migraine causes transient autonomic dysfunction resulting in gastroparesis (gastric paralysis) in up to 80% of attacks, even when nausea is mild.
Ingested oral pills pool in the stomach and do not reach the duodenal absorptive mucosa, leading to erratic absorption, delayed analgesia, and apparent treatment failure.
Bypassing oral route via nasal, subcutaneous, or rectal administration is essential.
