# Central Sensitization and Cutaneous Allodynia Timing

## Pathophysiology (Burstein et al., 2004)
1. **Phase 1: Peripheral Sensitization (0 - 60 min)**: First-order trigeminal vascular neurons are activated. Pulsatile headache, throbbing. Highly responsive to triptans.
2. **Phase 2: Central Sensitization (> 60 min)**: Second-order neurons in the trigeminocervical complex become hypersensitive. Cutaneous allodynia develops (pain from hair combing, touching face). Triptan efficacy plummets once central sensitization is established unless administered via non-oral routes with parenteral kinetics.
